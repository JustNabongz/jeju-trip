# Spain at Christmas 2026

Our nine-day trip, Friday 25 December 2026 to Saturday 2 January 2027, as a single web page: Barcelona, Valencia, Granada, Córdoba and Seville. It has the day-by-day plan with drive times, three to five food and coffee picks per meal, opening hours checked for each date, rain plans, drive briefings and the booking to-do list.

- **Online:** https://justnabongz.github.io/jeju-trip/spain/ is served by GitHub Pages from `main` of this repository. The page asks search engines not to list it.
- **Private details** (passenger names, booking codes and confirmation numbers) are not in this copy. They live only in the private version of the page.
- **Saving:** on this copy, ticks, picks and notes are saved in the browser you make them in.

Files: `index.html` is the app, `trip.json` the plan, `routes.json` the drive and walk times with road shapes, and `basemap.json` the map.
