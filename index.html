            Good to know

            Jeju basics for this trip.

            Drive times
            Each leg's time and distance comes from Naver Map's car directions with typical traffic. Weekends and the roads into Jeju City and Aewol run slower. Tap Route on any leg to open it in Kakao Map.
            Driving licence
            Most visitors need an International Driving Permit alongside their licence and passport to rent a car.
            Use Naver Map or Kakao Map to drive
            Google Maps is fine for looking places up but can't give driving directions in Korea. Naver and Kakao both announce the many speed cameras on Jeju's roads.
            No drinking and driving
            Korea's limit is 0.03% blood alcohol, and police run roadside checks, so save the soju for the Sheraton night when the car is parked.
            Ilchulbong at sunrise
            From October to February the gates open at 6:00 am, early enough to reach the rim for sunrise.
            Udo
            Rental cars can't go across unless you're staying on the island. Park at Seongsan Port, bring passports for the boarding form, and hire an e-bike there.
            Wind
            Pack a windproof layer. Coastal walks and ferries can close in strong wind or rough seas.
